FUZZY DRIFT

Drift fuzzy carts, ram your friends, watch them explode.

DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS

Version 1.0.0 · free · unzip and double-click FuzzyDrift.exe
If Windows says "protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.

Online arena

Unranked drops you into one arena with everyone playing. Drive, drift and collect coins together.

1v1 battles

Hit B to find a real opponent. Best of 3: ram them, knock them out of their cart, finish them off.

Ranked vs bots

Battle bots for rank points and climb from Bronze to Diamond.

Controls

  • WASD move / drive
  • W + A/D drift, let go to boost
  • Space jump
  • E get in / out of a cart
  • B find a 1v1 in the arena
  • F5 first / third person
  • Mouse: look (click to lock, Esc to free)
