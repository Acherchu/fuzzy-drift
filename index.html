Windows says it's a virus?

It isn't - Windows warns about every small game that isn't signed by a big company. Here's how to play anyway:

  1. If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded": open the downloads list, click the ... next to it and choose Keep.
  2. Unzip it: right-click the zip → Extract All.
  3. Double-click FuzzyDrift.exe. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Rather not? Just hit PLAY IN BROWSER - nothing to download at all.

Online arena

Unranked drops you into one arena with everyone playing. Drive, drift and collect coins together.

1v1 battles

Hit B to find a real opponent. Best of 3: ram them, knock them out of their cart, finish them off.

Ranked vs bots

Battle bots for rank points and climb from Bronze to Diamond.

Controls

  • WASD move / drive
  • W + A/D drift, let go to boost
  • Space jump
  • E get in / out of a cart
  • B find a 1v1 in the arena
  • V first / third person
  • Mouse: look (click to lock, Esc to free)
